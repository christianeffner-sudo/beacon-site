BEACON

KEEP THE LIGHT · GUIDE THE SHIPS HOME

A short, calm lighthouse game for iPhone. Questions, bugs or ideas? Write to christian.effner@googlemail.com – usually answered within a few days.

How to play

Tap Light the lamp, then drag your thumb across the sea: the beam follows it. Keep the light on a ship until it turns for the harbour channel; ships left in the dark run onto the reef. Between nights, spend your coins in the upgrade tree. After about four hours comes the last storm, then endless nights.

Questions

I don't hear any sound.

Beacon follows your iPhone's silent mode, so it never surprises you in a quiet room. Switch silent mode off (Ring/Silent switch or Action button) and check the volume. Sound effects and music can also be turned off separately on the Harbor map.

How do I restore the full game on a new iPhone?

Open the Harbor map and tap Restore purchase. Use the same Apple ID you bought it with. With Family Sharing, family members get it too.

Is my progress saved?

Yes, after every night and every purchase, on your iPhone. Deleting the app deletes the progress; the purchase can always be restored.

Does Beacon collect data or show ads?

No. No ads, no tracking, no accounts. See the privacy policy.

Can I get a refund?

Purchases are handled by Apple. Request a refund at reportaproblem.apple.com.
