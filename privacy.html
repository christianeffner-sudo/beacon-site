BEACON

Privacy Policy · last updated 11 November 2026

Short version: Beacon does not collect, store or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking.

Who is responsible

Beacon is made by Christian Effner, Breitlacherstraße 86, 60489 Frankfurt am Main, Germany. Contact: christian.effner@googlemail.com.

Data stored on your device

Your progress (coins, upgrades, records), your settings (sound, music, haptics) and whether you own the full game are saved only on your iPhone. They never leave the device and are deleted when you delete the app.

Purchases

The full game is bought through Apple's App Store. Apple handles the payment; we never see your name, payment details or Apple ID. Apple's privacy policy applies: apple.com/legal/privacy.

Game Center

If you are signed in to Game Center, Beacon submits your best single night to the leaderboard "Best night". That score is stored and shown by Apple under your Game Center name; we receive nothing. If you are not signed in, nothing is sent. You can sign out in the iPhone's Settings → Game Center.

No third parties

Beacon contains no analytics or advertising tools. It uses the internet only for the App Store purchase and Game Center, both handled by iOS.

Children

Because Beacon collects no personal data, it collects none from children either.

Your rights

Since we process no personal data about you, there is nothing for us to show, correct or delete. If you have questions, write to christian.effner@googlemail.com.

Changes

If Beacon ever starts processing data, this page will be updated before that version is released.
